Course Overview

ITBM Course Introduction

Industry Trends and Business Models (ITBM-EMI) is a premium English-Medium Instruction (EMI) course. Students explore global macroeconomic currents and corporate strategies by mastering core analytical frameworks, including the Industry Life Cycle, PESTEL, Value Chain, and Business Model Canvas. Through final Single Page Application (SPA) term projects, student teams conduct extensive case analyses of leading global giants like Nvidia, COSTCO (Costco Wholesale Corporation), and Delta, mapping theoretical insights to actionable industrial business models.
